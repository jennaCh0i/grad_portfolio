Sweetie — Start with one word
Sweetie is an AI-powered ordering experience for Sweetgreen that turns a single word — a craving, a mood, or a need — into a strong starting bowl, so customers never have to build one from scratch.
*Student project, not affiliated with Sweetgreen.
Role
UI designer
Timeline
10 weeks
Tools
Figma
Problem
Too much customization can turn choice into decision overload.
Today’s ordering flow asks customers to build every bowl ingredient by ingredient.
Current Sweetgreen in-store kiosk
Who is this for?
Customers who know what they want, but not exactly what to order — people in a hurry, new to the menu, working toward a dietary goal, or looking for a personalized bowl without building it from scratch.
Top task
Get a personalized bowl recommendation from a single keyword.
Sub tasks
Review the recommended bowl
Understand why the bowl was recommended
Check ingredients and nutrition
Explore ingredient sourcing
Customize the recommendation if needed

Micro-interactions
Small, soft motions that show the AI is working and confirm every choice.
Trending nudge
Pops out of the Sweetie button when there’s something to explore.
Store header
Location and hours slide in so you know where you’re ordering.
Keyword wheel
Keywords scroll into focus and blur as they leave.
Bowl ready
The hex breathes while the bowl is being built.
Ingredient amounts
Pills fill to show how much — and stop at the group limit.
Dietary toggles
Filters snap on with a check, so the choice is confirmed.
Design system
Fresh
Starts from what the user wants right now — their mood, craving, goal, or preference.
Curated
Thoughtfully selects and combines ingredients into a personalized recommendation.
Real
Builds trust by showing real nutrition, ingredients, and sourcing behind the recommendation.
