Details

Hermès Sponsored Project · 2D/3D Motion Graphics + Visual · 2025

Tools: Cinema 4D, After Effects, Illustrator

Overview

Access your personal escape wherever you are. Whether tucked in a book, slipped into a pocket, or packed in your bag, the Vivlio book holder brings comfort within reach—on a yacht, in the sky, or from the couch.

What I did

My deliverables

  1. Led the visual design across product developing a distinct color palette and custom illustrations
  2. Shaped the core storytelling through a mix of 2D and 3D design
  3. Built a functional design system in close collaboration with industrial designers
  4. Managed the design team and kept the creative process focused and efficient
  5. Worked hands-on with industrial designers to bring the product vision to life
  6. Designed the final window display and presentation to showcase the full experience
