Campaign · Motion Graphics

Overview

While twins share a bond, they aren’t always identical. It’s more than looking alike—it’s the special connection that makes them belong together. This campaign celebrates how Camper shoes, like twins, might be different in details but are always made to be side by side, bringing out the best in each other.
