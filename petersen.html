Overview

The Petersen Automotive Museum is an automobile museum located on Wilshire Boulevard alongside Museum Row in the Miracle Mile neighborhood of Los Angeles. One of the world’s largest collections, the Petersen Automotive Museum is a nonprofit organization specializing in automobile history and related educational programs.
